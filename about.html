Our Story

Prisha Speech Therapy and Hearing Clinic was born from a simple but powerful belief: every person deserves to communicate and hear clearly, regardless of their background or economic situation. Founded by Dr. Prashant and Dr. Mridula Rani, the clinic was built on the foundation of clinical excellence, genuine compassion, and a patient-first philosophy.

Located in Bawadia Kalan, Bhopal, we have grown into one of the most trusted speech and hearing care centers in Madhya Pradesh, serving patients from Bhopal and from as far as Itarshi, Raisen, and other parts of the state. Our clinic features a dedicated play zone for children, making therapy sessions engaging and comfortable for young patients.

We are also proud to be an official center under the Government of India’s ADIP scheme for cochlear implant therapy — a recognition of our clinical standards and our commitment to making advanced hearing care accessible to all.
