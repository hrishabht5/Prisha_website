Our Story

A husband and wife team, working with Bhopal families for 15 years

Prisha Speech Therapy and Hearing Clinic was founded by Dr. Mridula Rani and Dr. Prashant in Bawadia Kalan, Bhopal. What started as a small practice has grown into one of the most trusted speech and hearing care centres in Madhya Pradesh, serving patients from Bhopal and from as far as Itarshi, Raisen, and Sehore.

The clinic has a dedicated play zone for children, making therapy sessions engaging and comfortable for young patients. We are also an official centre under the Government of India’s ADIP scheme for cochlear implant therapy — a recognition of our clinical standards and our commitment to making advanced hearing care accessible to all.

Patients have described us as people who “give enough time, don’t rush, and explain everything clearly.” That’s not something we designed — it’s simply how Dr. Mridula and Dr. Prashant work. Registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India. (Add registration number when available.)

How We Work

Three things that shape every consultation, every therapy session, every interaction.

Listen before treating

Every session begins with understanding. We ask questions, we listen, and we involve families in the plan. A correct diagnosis matters more than a fast one.

Evidence-based methods

Our treatments follow current clinical research in audiology and speech-language pathology. We don’t guess — we test, measure, and adjust.

Support after the clinic

Therapy doesn’t end at the clinic door. We provide home plans, progress reviews, and remain reachable between sessions for patients who need guidance.
