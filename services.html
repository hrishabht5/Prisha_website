Service 02

Audiometry

Our comprehensive hearing assessments identify the type and degree of hearing loss quickly and accurately. All tests are painless, non-invasive, and carried out in a sound-treated environment with results explained on the same day.

We also offer BERA (Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry) — a specialised test for infants, young children, and cases where conventional audiometry is not possible. BERA can be performed even on sleeping babies as young as a few months old.

Early detection of hearing loss is critical — especially in children — to ensure proper speech and language development. We recommend hearing tests for anyone who suspects difficulty hearing, has ringing in the ears, or has a family history of hearing loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from patients and families. Don’t see your question? Contact us directly.

Speech therapy is recommended for children with delayed speech or language milestones, children who stammer or stutter, individuals with articulation disorders, adults who have experienced stroke or brain injury affecting communication, and people with voice disorders. If you or your child is having difficulty communicating clearly, a consultation can help determine if therapy is needed.

Newborn hearing screening is recommended at birth. If any concern arises, follow-up testing should happen as early as possible. For all children, a hearing evaluation is recommended if parents notice that the child is not responding to sounds, is slow to develop speech, or frequently asks for things to be repeated. Early detection leads to significantly better outcomes for speech and language development.

The number of sessions varies significantly depending on the type and severity of the disorder, the patient’s age, and how consistently home practice is done between sessions. Some patients see improvement in 8–12 sessions; others with more complex needs may require several months of ongoing therapy. No, audiometry is completely painless and non-invasive. The tests involve listening to tones through headphones and pressing a button when you hear them. For impedance/tympanometry, a soft probe tip is placed gently at the ear canal opening. The entire evaluation takes 30–45 minutes.

A BERA (Brainstem Evoked Response Audiometry) test is a specialised, painless hearing test used for infants, very young children, and patients who cannot respond to conventional testing. It measures the brain’s response to sound via small electrodes placed on the scalp. It can even be performed on a sleeping baby. At Prisha Clinic, we have conducted BERA tests on babies as young as 4 months old.

Coverage depends on your specific insurance policy. Some government health schemes and private insurance plans do provide partial or full coverage for hearing aids. For persons with disabilities, the Government of India’s ADIP scheme provides financial assistance for assistive devices including hearing aids and cochlear implants. Our team can help guide you through the available options during your consultation.

A hearing aid amplifies sounds so that a damaged ear can detect them more easily — it is a non-surgical device worn in or behind the ear. A cochlear implant is a surgically implanted device that directly stimulates the auditory nerve, bypassing damaged parts of the inner ear. Cochlear implants are typically recommended for individuals with severe-to-profound sensorineural hearing loss who do not benefit sufficiently from hearing aids. After implantation, a structured therapy program at a certified center like Prisha Clinic is essential for learning to interpret the new sound signals.
