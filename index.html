§ 03 How a visit works

From your first call to ongoing care.

No mystery, no surprise charges. Here is what happens, in order.

01 · CALL

Reach out

Call, WhatsApp or fill the form. We confirm a time the same day.

02 · VISIT

First consultation

30–45 minutes with Dr. Mridula or Dr. Prashant. ₹500. We listen first.

03 · TEST

Assess carefully

Hearing screen, language probe, or BERA — whatever your concern calls for.
