Do You or Your Child Need Help?

Recognizing the early signs of speech or hearing difficulties can make a life-changing difference. Check if any of these apply.

🗣 Signs of Speech/Language Issues

✓
Child not babbling by 12 months or not saying words by 16 months
✓
Difficulty pronouncing sounds or words clearly
✓
Stuttering or stammering when speaking
✓
Limited vocabulary compared to peers of the same age
✓
Difficulty understanding or following instructions
✓
Voice sounds hoarse, nasal, or unusual
✓
Loss of previously acquired language skills

🔊 Signs of Hearing Loss

✓
Frequently asking people to repeat themselves
✓
Difficulty hearing in noisy environments
✓
Turning up TV or phone volume much louder than others
✓
Ringing or buzzing sounds in the ears (tinnitus)
✓
Child does not respond to their name or loud sounds
✓
Ear pain, discharge, or recurring ear infections
✓
Delayed speech development in a young child

If any of these signs sound familiar — don’t wait.
