About Prisha Clinic

Founded by two specialists, dedicated to one goal

Prisha Speech Therapy and Hearing Clinic was founded by Dr. Mridula Rani and Dr. Prashant in Bawadia Kalan, Bhopal. Over 15 years, we’ve helped hundreds of families across Madhya Pradesh with speech therapy, audiometry, hearing aids, and cochlear implant rehabilitation.

We are an official ADIP certified cochlear implant center under the Government of India, and our clinic has a dedicated play zone for children.
